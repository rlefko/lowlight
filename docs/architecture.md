# Architecture

The SwiftUI app owns one ongoing listening session, native transport controls, an About sheet, and the accepted sound bank. The local `LowlightPlanning` package owns native song plans, note events, a shared transition clock, and intent checkpoints. `LowlightAudio` prepares guitar/piano/bass/percussion samples off the audio thread and renders them through its small C++ `CLowlightDSP` target; see the [tonal preparation](validation-tonal.md), [tonal voice](validation-voices.md), [bass](validation-bass.md), [percussion](validation-percussion.md), [rhythm bus](validation-rhythm.md), [linked harmonic compression](validation-glue.md), [stereo room](validation-room.md), [linked peak protection](validation-peak.md), [song rack fades](validation-fades.md), [quiet air](validation-air.md), and [song mix](validation-mix.md) evidence. The optional bus view applies configured song fades before filters/glue/room and retains separate guitar/piano, processed bass/drums, and kick duck control for the harmonic rack; both output views share one voice pool and continuous state. A separate fixed-storage processor applies harmonic focus and linked compression, delaying all bus fields by 399 frames and explicitly draining the finite endpoint. A causal fixed-storage stereo room follows compression, preserves rhythm alignment, and keeps its tail alive through continued zero input. A separate linked peak protector supports song/session ceilings, adds 160 frames of lookahead, and explicitly drains at end of input without requiring a known stream length. A finite quiet-air stage prepares whole-channel normalization off-thread, then replays compatible noise into the mix with fixed storage and shared absolute tonal fades. A song mix stage combines the aligned room/rhythm buses with ducking and prepared air, then applies HP32 before fixed loudness gain. A separate [body loudness meter](validation-loudness.md) consumes that stereo body during preparation with fixed storage and freshly initialized K-weighting; a [prepared fixed gain](validation-gain.md) then scales every song frame before song peak protection. A validated [song layout and body selector](validation-song-body.md) now streams the finite post-HP32 song into that measurement while excluding intro and outro from K-weighting. The [mixer song origin](validation-origins.md) starts voices directly at the same absolute frame used by the schedule, fades, and air; reset returns to that origin without reconstructing earlier voices or filters. Native DSP entry points share a [scoped numeric environment](validation-native-rounding.md): round to nearest, flush subnormals, and restore caller control/status registers. A separate synchronous [voice preparation scope](validation-preparation-environment.md) uses round-to-nearest with gradual underflow around sample preparation and complete mixer construction, including event Float conversion. Existing native calls temporarily enter their flushing scope and restore that preparation mode afterward. Planning and frame derivation remain separate contracts. A [finite song source](validation-song-source.md) now owns voices through the post-HP32 mix, removes glue startup, drains its finite endpoint, and resets every stage together using fixed scratch buffers. A [prepared song](validation-prepared-song.md) now measures that source off-thread, retains one fixed gain, and resets it for finite replay through song peak protection with its startup and drain aligned. The [native schedule entry point](validation-native-preparation.md) derives events, layout, fades, and realized controls inside the same preparation scope. An opt-in [shared preparation pool](validation-shared-preparation.md) admits at most two song racks and 64 combined voice slots before analysis. A finite initial-session pair adds overlap and global output protection; a rolling owner reuses that renderer for serialized rack replacement. There is no external package dependency; the small adapted random-generation subset retains its [upstream notices](third-party-code.md). Resource membership is explicit in the Xcode project and package manifest. Reference score imports preserve [original note lengths](validation-reference-timing.md) and offer [explicit local placement](validation-reference-placement.md) at an integer session origin; native plans retain their shared absolute endpoint clock.

A [session-planning owner](validation-session-planning.md) now runs complete timeline operations, checkpoint encoding/restoration, and current-schedule frame queries inside the preparation numeric scope. It commits copied state only after success and supplies the seeded finite-pair initializer. The original planning APIs remain available with their original caller-environment contract.

Responsibilities stay separate: deterministic Swift planner and event timeline, bank preparation, a small C/C++ DSP kernel, playback coordinator, persistence, and UI. Prefer a few modules with clear ownership. See the [native planning decision](adrs/002-native-planning.md) for algorithm versioning and bounded history, and the [shared-time decision](adrs/003-events-and-shared-time.md) for transitions and checkpoint limits.

Score preparation fully processes and validates each unique note, then retains only the tonal/bass prefix reachable by its longest event. Percussion keeps its complete shaped body. A [fixed 166-entry sample registry](validation-sample-capacity.md) covers the current normalized key domain independently of the 64 voice slots. The 2,048-event and 32 MiB PCM limits and explicit capacity failures remain in force; see [sample storage validation](validation-sample-storage.md).

The audio callback will render from preallocated storage only. No planning, decoding, allocation, locks, logging, I/O, async calls, or UI updates belong there. Plan one successor, use at most two song racks, and enforce 64 total voices with deterministic stealing. Handle arbitrary callback frame counts in bounded subblocks.

A [finite protected song pair](validation-song-pair.md) now privately prepares two adjacent initial-session schedules through one pool. Its native renderer places aligned song output on the shared frame clock, sums oldest first, applies session entry/exit fades, and removes the session peak protector's startup/drain delay. It retains both racks through EOF and reset. The finite endpoint must precede or equal the next transition's start; the rolling owner extends this path through retirement and replacement.

A [bounded rolling session](validation-rolling-session.md) keeps one session protector alive across any number of finite-song replacements. It pauses at the oldest full EOF, detaches and releases that rack off the render path, then admits its successor through the same pool. Failed preparation retries the same pending schedule while the survivor and protector remain paused. The default mode serializes calls. Opt-in [concurrent preparation](validation-concurrent-preparation.md) lets a separate render endpoint continue the survivor while one worker releases the retired rack and prepares its successor. A bounded native acquire/release handoff transfers borrowed pointers without callback reclamation; late preparation freezes at the missing onset. Both roles must stop and join before destruction. [Cooperative preparation cancellation](validation-cancellation.md) now lets a control thread signal one worker attempt without touching live render state. The worker observes the request during setup or between analysis blocks, releases unfinished resources, and retains the same pending successor for a later retry. The app uses the same renderer for continuous device playback.

A [managed preparation worker](validation-preparation-worker.md) now owns one dedicated thread for initial construction and automatic successor preparation. It polls the existing atomic handoff off the callback, latches failures until explicit retry, and cooperatively cancels the current attempt on terminal stop. It supports [reversible suspension](validation-preparation-suspension.md): cooperatively cancel the active attempt, acknowledge quiescence, and wait without polling until resume or stop. Resume preserves the pending schedule and latched failures. Its render endpoint is unchanged. Joining this worker only joins preparation; the caller must separately stop/join the consumer and release both owners off the callback.

Preserve the canonical 32 kHz signal path and convert at the output boundary. Keep effect and limiter state continuous. Offline rendering must exercise production DSP. Whole-song gain analysis needs a bounded-memory pass off the callback; its startup and CPU costs must be measured.

Persist versioned musical intent atomically off the callback. Restoring a checkpoint does not restore DSP state. Relaunch must stay silent until Play, then restore gracefully with a fade.

A [session audio source](validation-audio-source.md) now bridges the managed worker to an `AVAudioSourceNode`. Its fixed 32 kHz interleaved Float32 block renders directly into supplied storage; Apple handles supported PCM conversion at the output connection. A bounded atomic status exposes waiting, EOF, and faults without involving the worker lock. The node pins its worker and endpoint until the host has quiesced rendering and releases it off the callback. A [session audio engine owner](validation-audio-engine.md) now controls graph start, pause/resume, and terminal cleanup on a serialized non-main context. It pauses output before suspending preparation and stops output before joining and releasing both owners. An [observable main-actor coordinator](validation-playback-coordination.md) now lazily constructs that owner, coalesces intent and snapshot delivery, cancels obsolete startup, and dispatches all engine work and final release to one serial control queue. Refresh never authorizes playback, and terminal cleanup is acknowledged after the queue releases the old owners. The coordinator now owns the [iOS playback audio-session lifecycle](validation-audio-session.md), activating only after preparation and deactivating after output is quiescent. It handles [interruptions and output disconnection](validation-playback-interruptions.md) through that same mailbox, preserving user intent and allowing only permitted automatic recovery. Its [engine configuration recovery](validation-engine-configuration.md) reconnects the retained source at the new output rate while preserving musical state and user intent. A separate [media-services recovery](validation-media-services.md) path invalidates orphaned system objects and requires fresh user playback intent. The [continuous listening integration](validation-playback-integration.md) connects app and remote transport, prepared phrase-boundary Next, background audio, and bounded musical-position restoration. Its explicit ongoing mode uses 64-bit session clocks, while finite rendering retains its original contract. Saved intent belongs to the rendered selection at the rhythm handoff, not the ahead-of-playback planner. App file work and coalesced saves stay off the callback and do not grant autoplay permission. [Planning checkpoints](validation-checkpoint-persistence.md) can be saved and loaded explicitly without starting playback.
