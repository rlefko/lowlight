# Engineering conventions

- Keep the current milestone focused; consult docs/status.md before expanding scope.
- Use American English and system-native accessible UI. Do not claim audio or device validation that has not run.
- Preserve the accepted audio baseline and separate licensed asset provenance.
- Keep real-time rendering bounded and allocation-free; see docs/architecture.md.
- Run scripts/quality and relevant build/tests. Preserve partial staging and unrelated work.
- Keep generated build outputs and long recordings outside version control.
